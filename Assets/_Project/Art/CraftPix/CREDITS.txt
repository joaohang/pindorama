Assets used in this prototype:
- CraftPix 32x32 platformer tileset (colors and props)
- Objects: doors, button, lever, chest, spikes, target, training dummy
- Pipes

The original pack's tile/prefab/backtile PNGs contain size labels. The tiles in
Generated/Tilesets are a clean 9-slice derivative made from the CraftPix
palette; the label-free objects and pipes are used directly.
